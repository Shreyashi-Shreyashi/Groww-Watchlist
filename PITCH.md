## Pitch (100 words)

Most watchlists show today's price and call it done. Ours asks: what
changed since you last looked? Each symbol gets a persisted baseline from
your last visit; on return, we diff against it using four explainable
rules — price move past a threshold, volume spike, new high/low, reversal —
rather than an un-auditable score. The baseline updates only when you
leave, not every refresh, so the diff stays meaningful. One feed
subscription serves every viewer of a symbol, and stale data is flagged,
never hidden. State follows you across devices via a server-side record.
